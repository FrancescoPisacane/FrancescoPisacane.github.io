About

I study Mechatronics and Robotics at Politecnico di Milano, after a bachelor's in Automation and Control Engineering. My work sits between control theory, learning-based methods and real robots: state estimation, system identification, SLAM, reinforcement learning and reduced-order modelling.

In 2025–2026 I spent a semester at Ghent University, took part in the IDEA League Challenge Programme with study periods at ETH Zurich, TU Delft, RWTH Aachen and Chalmers, and then moved to NASA JPL in Pasadena for seven months of research.

Outside university I fly a custom-built drone, shoot aerial video for real estate agencies, and hike whenever the weather allows it.
